🎧 Using on iPhone/iPad

Option 1: Open in Safari (Easiest)

1 Share the URL to yourself
On your Mac, run: python3 -m http.server 8000
Then text yourself: http://YOUR-MAC-IP:8000
2 Open link in Safari on iPhone
Tap the link you sent yourself
3 Start listening!
Tap the play button ▶️

Option 2: Add to Home Screen (Best Experience)

1 Open in Safari
Follow steps above to open the player
2 Tap Share button
Tap ⬆️ at the bottom of Safari
3 Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4 Name it and Add
Change name to "UX News" (or whatever you like) and tap "Add"
5 Launch from home screen
Now it works like a real app! Opens in full screen without Safari bars.

Option 3: Host Online (Access Anywhere)

Deploy to Netlify (Free):
  1. Create account at netlify.com
  2. Drag the audio_player folder to Netlify
  3. Get a URL like: https://ux-news.netlify.app
  4. Access from anywhere!

iPhone Tips

🎯 Best practices:
  • Use Safari - Chrome doesn't have speech synthesis on iOS
  • Keep volume up - Check ringer/media volume
  • First tap - iOS requires user gesture for first play
  • Background play - Works with screen locked!
  • AirPods - Connect before playing for best quality

Finding Your Mac's IP Address

On Mac:
System Settings → Network → Wi-Fi → Details
Look for "IP Address": Usually like 192.168.1.x

Troubleshooting

No sound?
  • Check ringer switch on side of iPhone
  • Turn up volume with buttons
  • Tap play button twice
  • Force close Safari and reopen
Won't load?
  • Make sure iPhone and Mac on same Wi-Fi
  • Check Mac firewall isn't blocking port 8000
  • Try typing IP address manually in Safari
Choppy playback?
  • Close other apps
  • Move closer to Wi-Fi router
  • Consider hosting online instead
